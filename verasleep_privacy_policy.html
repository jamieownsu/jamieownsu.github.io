VeraSleep Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 27, 2026

Note: Translations are provided for convenience only. In the event of any discrepancy or conflict in meaning, the original English version of this Privacy Policy shall govern.

VeraSleep (“VeraSleep”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) helps parents and caregivers log a baby’s sleep and meals, see patterns, and share that log with a partner. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain and short.

1. Who we are

VeraSleep is operated by Jamie O'Toole, doing business as Chalupin Software, based in Ontario, Canada. Jamie O'Toole is the person responsible for the protection of personal information. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to chalupinsoftware@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

WhatDetails
Account information When you sign in, we receive an account identifier and, depending on the sign-in method you choose (Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple), your email address and display name. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we receive Apple’s private relay address instead. You can change your display name in the app.
Baby profile Your baby’s name, sex and birth date, which the app uses to tailor its predictions, and the settings you choose for that baby (when night starts and how many naps per day). We recommend using a first name or nickname.
Sleep and meal logs Nap and sleep start and end times, meal times and amounts, the first name and account identifier of the person who started each sleep, and the predictions and insights calculated from them.
Sharing information If you share a baby, we store who has access to it and their display names, and a one-time join code (shown as a QR code). Join codes are only valid for a few minutes and are deleted automatically afterwards, or as soon as someone is removed from the baby.
Notifications If you allow notifications, we store an identifier for each of your devices (a Firebase installation ID), the device’s platform and language, and your notification choices for each baby you share (naps and night sleep). Notifications you receive contain the baby’s name, whether a sleep started or ended, its duration, and the first name of the person who started it.
Subscription information Whether you have an active trial or subscription, and when it ends. Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we do not receive or store your payment card details.
Usage and diagnostics Usage events, crash reports, and device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, and an app-instance identifier), used to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. This information is not linked to your name or email, but it is tied to an app-instance identifier rather than being fully anonymous.
Camera The camera is used only when you choose to scan a QR code to join a shared baby. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone audio.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the app: record and display your baby’s sleep and meals, calculate nap predictions and insights, and back up and sync your data across your devices.
  • To let you share a baby with a partner and keep both of your logs in sync.
  • If you turn notifications on, to let you know when another caregiver starts or ends a sleep for a baby you share.
  • To manage your trial and subscription and unlock the features you are entitled to.
  • To measure and improve the app, and to find and fix crashes and bugs.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where your data is stored and who processes it

Your data is stored on your device and, once you are signed in, backed up to our cloud database so it can sync and be restored. We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check, Cloud Messaging): sign-in, data storage and sync, app configuration, usage analytics, crash reporting, delivering notifications, and checking that requests come from the genuine app on a genuine device.
  • Apple Push Notification service: delivers notifications to iPhones, if you allow them.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: if you choose to sign in with your Google or Apple account.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions and entitlements, working with Google Play and the Apple App Store.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store: process purchases under their own privacy policies.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. We share only what is needed for them to provide their service.

5. Sharing with a partner

The account that creates a baby is its owner, and can share it with up to four other people. Everyone who shares a baby can see its profile and its sleep and meal logs, and the display names of the others who share it. While the owner has an active trial or subscription, everyone can add and edit logs; otherwise the baby is read-only for everyone. Only the owner can change the baby’s details or remove people.

Only share with people you trust. The owner can remove a person at any time, and anyone who has joined can leave. If the owner deletes their account, the baby and its logs are deleted for everyone who shares it.

If the others who share a baby have notifications turned on, they are notified when you start or end a sleep, with your first name on sleeps you start. This is the same information they can already see in the app.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data for as long as your account exists so the app can work and sync.
  • Remove a baby: removes that baby and its logs from the app on every device that shares it. The records are marked as removed on our servers and are permanently erased when you delete your account, or sooner if you ask us by email.
  • Notifications: a device’s identifier is removed when you sign out on that device, and all of your device identifiers and notification choices are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Leave a shared baby: removes that shared baby’s data from your device and your access to it. The baby and its logs, including the sleeps you logged and your first name on them, remain with its owner.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Account and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the data associated with it, including the baby profiles, sleep logs and meal logs you own, for everyone they are shared with. You are also removed from any babies others shared with you, and your first name and account identifier are removed from the sleeps you logged for them, including babies you had already left. The logs themselves stay with their owners. Some entries logged with versions of the app released before November 25, 2026 may keep your first name; email us and we will remove it. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke VeraSleep’s access to your Apple account. Our servers finish this clean-up shortly after you confirm.

Backups and diagnostic data held by our providers may persist for a short period after deletion before they are removed in the normal course. If you cannot access the app, email us and we will help.

How to delete your account and data

In the app: open the Account tab, choose Delete account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and the baby profiles, sleep logs and meal logs you own. To remove only one baby, use Remove baby on that baby instead (see above for how its records are erased).

Without the app: email chalupinsoftware@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (or tell us which Google or Apple account you used to sign in), and we will delete your account and associated data. We aim to complete requests within 30 days. Active subscriptions are managed by Google Play or the App Store and must be cancelled there.

7. Your choices and rights

You can access, correct, and delete your information in the app. Depending on where you live (for example under Canadian privacy law, the GDPR, or the CCPA), you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw consent, or object to certain processing. To make a request, contact chalupinsoftware@gmail.com. You can also limit analytics collection through your device’s advertising and privacy settings, and turn notifications on or off for each baby in the app’s Settings or for the whole app in your device’s settings.

8. Children’s privacy

VeraSleep is intended for parents and caregivers aged 18 or older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Information about a baby is entered by the parent or caregiver, who is responsible for it. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Not medical advice

Nap predictions and insights are general guidance based on the information you log. They are not medical advice and do not replace the advice of your pediatrician or healthcare provider.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with reputable cloud providers using access rules that limit who can read it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
