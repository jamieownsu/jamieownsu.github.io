Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Note: Translations are provided for convenience only. In the event of any discrepancy or conflict in meaning, the original English version of this Privacy Policy shall govern.

Chalupin Software ("we," "us," or "our") operates the VeraSleep mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide baby sleep tracking, feeding logs, cloud backup, and caregiver syncing:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your authentication identifier, display name, and email address via Firebase Authentication.
  • Child & Care Log Data: You may enter your child's name, birth date, sex, sleep sessions (naps and nighttime sleep), and feeding logs. This data is stored locally on your device and synced to Google Cloud Firestore so you can back up your records and share access with invited caregivers.
  • Partner Sharing Data: When you invite a partner or caregiver to a child's profile, your display name and user ID are shared with authorized members of that profile so logs stay synchronized across devices.
  • Subscription & Purchase History: We use RevenueCat, Apple App Store, and Google Play to process and validate subscriptions. We receive anonymous purchase receipts, subscription status, and entitlement records, but we never have access to your credit card or payment details.
  • Diagnostics & Usage Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymized crash reports, device diagnostics, and general feature usage statistics to improve app stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and synchronize your child's sleep, feeding, and schedule predictions across your devices and invited caregivers.
  • To verify active free trials and subscriptions.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve App performance.
  • To respond to customer support inquiries.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers to operate the App. These providers process data strictly on our behalf and in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics): Cloud database hosting, user authentication, and diagnostics.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription status and receipt validation.
  • Apple App Store & Google Play: App distribution and in-app billing.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or your child's care logs to third parties, and we do not display third-party behavioral advertising in the App.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Your account and care logs are retained for as long as your account remains active. You can delete your account and associated cloud data at any time directly within the App's account settings, or by emailing us at chalupinsoftware@gmail.com. Upon deletion, your active profile and logs are permanently removed from our live database (and any encrypted rolling backups expire automatically within their scheduled retention window).

5. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and our cloud servers is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS, and data stored in Google Cloud Firestore is encrypted at rest and protected by strict user-level access controls.

6. Children's Privacy

VeraSleep is designed solely for use by parents and adult caregivers. We do not knowingly allow children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) to create accounts or submit personal information directly through the App.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data access or deletion, please contact us at chalupinsoftware@gmail.com.
