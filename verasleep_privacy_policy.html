VeraSleep Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

VeraSleep (“VeraSleep”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) helps parents and caregivers log a baby’s sleep and meals, see patterns, and share that log with a partner. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain and short.

1. Who we are

VeraSleep is operated by Jamie O'Toole, based in Ontario, Canada. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to chalupinsoftware@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

WhatDetails
Account information When you sign in, we receive an account identifier and, depending on the sign-in method you choose, your email address and display name (for example from Google Sign-In).
Baby profile The name you give your baby and any details you choose to enter, such as a birth date. We recommend using a first name or nickname.
Sleep and meal logs Nap and sleep start and end times, meal times and amounts, and the predictions and insights calculated from them.
Sharing information If you share a baby with a partner, we store who has access, and the one-time code or QR code used to join.
Subscription information Whether you have an active trial or subscription, and when it ends. Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we do not receive or store your payment card details.
Usage and diagnostics Anonymous usage events, crash reports, and device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, and an app-instance identifier), used to understand how the app is used and to fix problems.
Camera The camera is used only when you choose to scan a QR code to join a shared baby. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone audio.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the app: record and display your baby’s sleep and meals, calculate nap predictions and insights, and back up and sync your data across your devices.
  • To let you share a baby with a partner and keep both of your logs in sync.
  • To manage your trial and subscription and unlock the features you are entitled to.
  • To measure and improve the app, and to find and fix crashes and bugs.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where your data is stored and who processes it

Your data is stored on your device and, once you are signed in, backed up to our cloud database so it can sync and be restored. We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics): sign-in, data storage and sync, app configuration, usage analytics, and crash reporting.
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with your Google account.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions and entitlements, working with Google Play and the Apple App Store.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store: process purchases under their own privacy policies.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. We share only what is needed for them to provide their service.

5. Sharing with a partner

When you share a baby, the person who joins can see and (while the owner has an active trial or subscription) add and edit that baby’s sleep and meal logs. Only share with people you trust. You can stop sharing, or remove the baby, at any time from within the app.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data for as long as your account exists so the app can work and sync.
  • Remove a baby: deletes that baby’s sleep and meal logs from the app and our servers.
  • Leave a shared baby: removes that shared baby’s data from your device.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Account and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the data associated with it, including baby profiles, sleep logs and meal logs you own.

Backups and diagnostic data held by our providers may persist for a short period after deletion before they are removed in the normal course. If you cannot access the app, email us and we will help.

How to delete your account and data

In the app: open the Account tab, choose Delete account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and the baby profiles, sleep logs and meal logs you own. To delete only one baby’s data, use Remove baby on that baby instead.

Without the app: email chalupinsoftware@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (or tell us which Google or Apple account you used to sign in), and we will delete your account and associated data. We aim to complete requests within 30 days. Active subscriptions are managed by Google Play or the App Store and must be cancelled there.

7. Your choices and rights

You can access, correct, and delete your information in the app. Depending on where you live (for example under Canadian privacy law, the GDPR, or the CCPA), you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw consent, or object to certain processing. To make a request, contact chalupinsoftware@gmail.com. You can also limit analytics collection through your device’s advertising and privacy settings.

8. Children’s privacy

VeraSleep is intended for parents and caregivers aged 18 or older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Information about a baby is entered by the parent or caregiver, who is responsible for it. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Not medical advice

Nap predictions and insights are general guidance based on the information you log. They are not medical advice and do not replace the advice of your pediatrician or healthcare provider.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with reputable cloud providers using access rules that limit who can read it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
